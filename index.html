How to play
  • Put 8 dachshunds on the board.
  • Each row, each column and each coloured territory gets exactly one dog.
  • Dogs can't touch each other, not even diagonally. They need room to stretch!
  • Tap / left-click marks a square with ✕ (tap again to clear it).
  • Double-tap / right-click places a dog. (You can also switch the tap mode to “Dog”.)
  • Placing a dog in the wrong spot costs a ❤. Lose all 3 and the game ends.
  • Every puzzle has exactly one solution, and you can always reach it by logic. You never need to guess.
  • Stuck? 🦴 Hint highlights the next logical step and explains why it works.
  • Tip: look for small territories, or territories that fit in just one row or column.
  • Keyboard: arrow keys move, X/Space marks, D/Enter places a dog, Z undoes.
