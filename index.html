How to play
  • Put 8 dachshunds on the board.
  • Each row, each column and each coloured territory gets exactly one dog.
  • Dogs can't touch each other, not even diagonally.
  • Tap a square to mark it ✕. Drag across squares to mark lots at once (drag from an ✕ to erase).
  • Double-tap / right-click places a dog. Or switch the tap mode to “Dog”.
  • A wrong dog costs a ❤. So does marking ✕ on a square where a dog belongs, and that dog is then revealed. Lose all 3 and you'll need to try again.
  • Every level has exactly one solution, and you can always reach it by logic. You never need to guess.
  • A game is 50 levels that get harder as you go. Every new game deals brand-new puzzles, made just for you. Finish all 50 for your total score, then start the next game.
  • A free square has no ✕ and isn't in line with, in the same territory as, or touching a dog you've placed.
  • 🦴 Hint shows the next logical step on the board and explains it.
  • ♾️ Endless mode (in Levels): random puzzles that get harder as you go. Hearts carry over between puzzles, and a flawless solve wins one back. How far can you get?
  • Score: be quick, keep your hearts, skip the hints, and use as few ✕s as you can. Finishing with no ✕s at all earns a big bonus.
  • Your progress, scores and badges are saved only on this device, in this browser. They aren't sent anywhere. Clearing your browser's site data erases them.
  • Keyboard: arrow keys move, X/Space marks, D/Enter places a dog, Z undoes, H gives a hint.
